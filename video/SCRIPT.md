# What to say — word for word (≈ 2:15)

Speak slowly: about 2.5 words a second. `[ ]` = read it off the screen on the
day, never guess. Numbers in sections 1–2 are sourced (bottom of the page).

## 1 · Hook — on camera, at a real step or narrow door (0:00–0:15)

> "This step is fifteen centimetres high.
> For me, it's nothing. For someone in a wheelchair, this building ends here.
> And the worst part? Someone signed off on this plan."

*(Measure the step for real. If it's a door, say "This door is [xx] centimetres wide.")*

## 2 · The problem — over the `Problem` animation (0:15–0:45)

> "One point three billion people live with a significant disability. That's one in six of us.
> By 2050, one person in six on Earth will be over sixty-five.
> Yet in the United States, fewer than one home in a hundred is built for a wheelchair.
> In England, it's under one in ten that a wheelchair user can even visit.
> This isn't one country's problem. It's how we design homes everywhere."

## 3 · Why it happens — over `HandCheck` (0:45–1:00)

> "Today, an engineer checks every plan by hand. Room by room, with a ruler.
> It takes hours. And when you're tired, you miss one.
> Once it's built, the mistake is in the concrete."

## 4 · Solution — on camera, then `Intro` (1:00–1:10)

> "So we built ConformScope. You give it a plan, and in [under a minute] it tells you
> whether a wheelchair can actually live there."

## 5 · Demo — over `DemoCut`, the sample plan (1:10–2:05)

| Screen | Say |
|---|---|
| Drop the PDF | "Here's a real apartment plan. I just drop it in." |
| Progress | "Meta's Muse Spark reads the room names and the scale. SAM 3.1 outlines every room." |
| Rooms appear | "Then geometry decides. In every room, we find the largest free circle. Green: a one-fifty turn fits. Red: it doesn't." |
| Click the red room | "This [WC] tops out at [one forty-six]. It needs one fifty. It fails — by [four] centimetres." |
| Play the walk | "Now we walk the plan, like a wheelchair would. And it stops — right here, where a real person would be stuck." |
| Fix it / Explain | "It explains why, and how to fix it. And a second model, Muse Spark Open, double-checks every verdict." |
| Merge a nook | "If the plan is unclear, the engineer corrects it in one click." |
| Export PDF | "And one click gives a report, ready for the client or the inspector." |

## 6 · Close — on camera (2:05–2:15)

> "[Hours] of checking, now [under a minute]. And mistakes caught on paper, not in concrete.
> ConformScope. Because every home should have room to turn around."

*(Say the time saving only as you measured it: your stopwatch on the app vs. a
real hand check of the same plan. No measurement → drop that line.)*

## Sources for section 2

- 1.3 billion / 1 in 6: WHO, *Global report on health equity for persons with disabilities*, 2022.
- 1 in 6 over 65 by 2050: UN DESA, *World Population Prospects 2019* (up from 1 in 11 in 2019).
- US < 1 %: HUD, *Accessibility of America's Housing Stock* (2011 AHS), 2015.
- England 9 %: *English Housing Survey 2018-19*, accessibility of English homes ("visitable" = all four features).
