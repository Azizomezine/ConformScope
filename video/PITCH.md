# Live demo — word for word (≈ 2:30)

**Bold** = what you do. `>` = what you say. `[ ]` = read it off the screen, never guess.
Start on the landing page, at the top. Speak slowly, and stop talking while something moves.

## Part 1 · The landing page — 6 steps (≈ 1:00)

**Mouse still on the title.**

> "Welcome to ConformScope, where we ask one fundamental question:
> can a wheelchair live in this home?
> We answer it in six steps. Let me show you."

**Click "See how it works".** The 3D scene starts and the step rail appears on the side.
Scroll gently. If you overshoot, click the step on the rail.

| # | Step on the rail | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reading** | Scroll to "Reading the plan" | "Step one: we read the plan. Muse Spark, Meta's model, reads the room names and the scale." |
| 2 | **Rooms** | Scroll | "Step two: SAM 3.1 cuts out every room, corner by corner." |
| 3 | **Volume** | Scroll, pause 2 s | "Step three: the walls go up. Now it's a home, not a drawing." |
| 4 | **Circle** | Scroll | "Step four: the rule. In every room, a wheelchair must be able to turn around. That's a circle of one metre fifty." |
| 5 | **Verdict** | Scroll, pause 2 s on the WC | "Step five: the verdict. The WC only fits one forty. A wheelchair can't turn in it." |
| 6 | **The fix** | Scroll | "Step six: the fix. Move one wall ten centimetres. On paper, that's free. In concrete, it's a renovation." |

> "And the verdict comes from geometry, not from the AI. The models read the plan; the maths measures it.
> Now let's do it for real."

**Scroll to the bottom and click "Check a plan →".**

## Part 2 · Checking a real plan (≈ 1:30)

| # | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Drag the PDF onto "Open a plan"** *(backup: **Try the sample plan**)* | "Here's a real apartment plan. I just drop it in." |
| 2 | **Hands off** while the status line runs | "Muse Spark reads the names and the scale, SAM 3.1 outlines the rooms, and everything is measured in metres." |
| 3 | **Click "Whole sheet"** | "Every room, measured. Green: a wheelchair can turn. Red: it can't." |
| 4 | In *Rooms*, **click "Fail"**, then **click the red room**. Hold the mouse on the circle. | "This [WC] tops out at [one forty-six]. It needs one fifty. It fails by [four] centimetres." |
| 5 | **Click "Report"** → *Walk the plan* → **"Play the walk"**. Say nothing until it stops. | "Now a wheelchair walks the home from the front door… and it stops. Right here." |
| 6 | **Click "Fix it: …"** or **"Explain the failures"** | "It tells you why it fails and how to fix it, and a second model, Muse Spark Open, double-checks it." |
| 7 | **Click "Export the report (PDF)"**, open it, scroll one page | "And one click gives a report, ready for the client or the inspector." |

**Close — look at the audience:**

> "ConformScope catches it on paper, not in concrete.
> Because every home should have room to turn around."

## Before you go live

- Rehearse once on the same plan and note its real numbers for Part 2, step 4.
  The Ø1.40 WC in Part 1 belongs to the landing page's demo apartment, not to your plan.
- Clear old corrections on that plan, and check that step 6 says *Verified by Muse Spark Open*.
- If the upload or Wi-Fi fails, click **Try the sample plan** and carry on.
- If it's slow at step 2: "It's reading a real scan, so it takes a few seconds."
